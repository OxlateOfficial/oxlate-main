# BRAND VOICE — OXLATE V1

Version: 1.0  
Status: ACTIVE  
Last Updated: 2026-09-10

## Voice Profile
- **Tone:** Architectural, restrained, disciplined, articulate.
- **Perspective:** An experienced software craft studio talking peer-to-peer with founders and product teams.
- **Sound Like:** An architectural monographs editor combined with a principal systems engineer.
- **Do Not Sound Like:** An aggressive sales agency, a breathless tech startup, or a generic freelance portfolio.

## Example Expressions
- *Instead of:* "We are the best web development agency that crushes your competition!"
- *Use:* "Oxlate is a precision technology partner building software with calm, architectural discipline."
- *Instead of:* "Book your free 30-minute growth strategy discovery call now!"
- *Use:* "Have something worth building? Start a conversation."
