## 1. Micro-Interaction Rules (`DECIDED`)

- **Primary Bronze CTA:** On hover, background transitions to Warm Bronze (`#B98250`); -1px subtle vertical lift over 150ms ease-out.
- **Secondary Links & Nav Anchors:** Text shifts from Neutral Slate (`#6C6B66`) to Deep Black (`#111111`) or Bronze (`#A87445`); subtle hairline indicator appears.
- **Showcase Project Cards:** Border contrast elevates from Pale Stone (`#D9D4CC`) to Deep Black (`#111111`) or Bronze (`#A87445`); image scale is restricted to `scale-[1.01]` max over 200ms ease-out.
- **Restraint:** No aggressive `scale-110` pop animations or 3D tilt flips.
