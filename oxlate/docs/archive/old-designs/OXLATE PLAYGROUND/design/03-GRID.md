## 1. Architectural Grid Logic (`DECIDED`)

Layouts are governed by architectural blueprint logic rather than fluid SaaS templates:

- **Maximum Content Container:** `1280px` (`80rem`), centered with generous outer margins.
- **Desktop Grid (`≥ 1024px`):** 12 columns with `2rem` (32px) gutters and `3rem` (48px) side margins.
- **Tablet Grid (`768px – 1023px`):** 8 columns with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters and `2rem` (32px) side margins.
- **Mobile Grid (`< 768px`):** 4 columns with `1rem` (16px) gutters and `1.25rem` (20px) side margins.

---

## 2. Datum Scoring & Grid linework (`RECOMMENDATION`)

- **Horizontal Datum Rules:** Top of every section is introduced with a continuous `1px` Pale Stone rule (`#D9D4CC`).
- **Vertical Drafting Linework:** Delicate hairline vertical guides (0.5px or 1px stroke at 10–18% opacity) run through the background, subtle enough to simulate drafting paper without impeding text readability.
- **Zero Horizontal Overflow:** No transform translation, SVG path, or canvas element may cause horizontal scrollbars on any viewport.
