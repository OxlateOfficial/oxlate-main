## 1. Material Analog Vocabulary (`DECIDED`)

Oxlate UI surfaces mirror tactile architectural materials:
- **Parchment Surface:** Warm Ivory base environment (`#F5F2EC`).
- **Recessed Plane:** Soft Cream panel surface (`#ECE8E1`).
- **Elevated Card Surface:** Pure Parchment card fill (`#FAF8F5`).
- **Blackened Steel:** Dense Deep Black typography and frame geometry (`#111111`).
- **Graphite Linework:** Hairline Pale Stone scoring rules (`#D9D4CC`).
- **Machined Bronze:** Fine metallic hardware focal points (`#A87445`).

---

## 2. Tectonic Corner Radii (`DECIDED`)

- **Full Sections & Outer Containers:** `0px` (Architectural flush boundary).
- **Cards & Showcase Frames:** `2px – 6px` (Sharp, tectonic rectangular geometry).
- **Buttons & Action Triggers:** `2px – 4px` (Crisp solid geometry).
- **Banned Geometries:** Large 16px–24px rounded bubble cards, circular blob containers, and soft pill cards are strictly prohibited.

---

## 3. Shadows & Surface Depth (`DECIDED`)

- Fuzzy, heavy drop shadows are banned.
- Depth is achieved through **plane contrast, hairline borders, and subtle ambient occlusion** (`box-shadow: 0 4px 20px rgba(17,17,17,0.03)`).
