## 1. Font Family Allocation (`DECIDED`)

- **Brand Identity & Technical Notation:** `Orbitron` / Vector Lettering (Strict restraint: brand wordmark, section numbers, technical coordinate tags).
- **Headlines & Display (H1, H2):** `Geist Sans` (or `Satoshi` if activated) — crisp, geometric, disciplined.
- **Body & Interface Copy:** `Geist Sans` — neutral, highly legible, calm.
- **Monospace Metadata:** `Geist Mono` — section tags, timestamps, code chips, tags.

---

## 2. Dynamic Typographic Scale (`RECOMMENDATION`)

Typographic hierarchy adapts smoothly across viewports:

- **Display / H1:**
  - Desktop (`≥ 1024px`): `3.5rem – 4.5rem` (56–72px), line-height `1.08`, tracking `-0.03em`.
  - Tablet (`768px – 1023px`): `2.75rem – 3.25rem` (44–52px).
  - Mobile (`< 768px`): `2.25rem – 2.5rem` (36–40px), line-height `1.15`, text-wrap balance.
- **Section Headlines (H2):**
  - Desktop: `2.25rem – 3.0rem` (36–48px).
  - Mobile: `1.75rem – 2.0rem` (28–32px).
- **Subtitles & Lead Copy:**
  - Desktop: `1.125rem – 1.25rem` (18–20px), line-height `1.55`.
  - Mobile: `1.0rem` (16px), line-height `1.5`.
- **Monospace Coordinates & Eyebrows:**
  - Uppercase `Geist Mono`, size `0.6875rem` (11px), tracking `+0.2em`.

---

## 3. Typographic Invariants (`DECIDED`)

- Headings are never rendered entirely in Bronze. Bronze is reserved strictly for micro-accents and active links.
- Typography creates visual authority through surrounding whitespace, not through drop shadows or gradients.
