## 1. Principles of Spacing (`DECIDED`)

- Whitespace is an active structural material, not blank emptiness.
- Generous spacing around headlines and CTAs produces an expensive editorial cadence.

---

## 2. Section Vertical Padding Scale (`RECOMMENDATION`)

- **Hero Viewport Stage:**
  - Desktop (`≥ 1024px`): Multi-stage scroll container (`100vh` to `160vh` controlled scroll progression).
  - Mobile (`< 768px`): Natural viewport fit (`min-h-[85vh]` to `100vh`), zero scroll traps.
- **Content Sections (Capabilities, Work, Approach, About, Contact):**
  - Desktop: `6rem – 8rem` (96px – 128px) top and bottom padding.
  - Mobile: `4rem – 5rem` (64px – 80px) top and bottom padding.
- **Component Intervals:**
  - Eyebrow tag to H2: `0.75rem – 1.0rem` (12px – 16px).
  - H2 to lead paragraph: `1.0rem – 1.5rem` (16px – 24px).
  - Lead paragraph to content grid: `3.0rem – 4.0rem` (48px – 64px).
