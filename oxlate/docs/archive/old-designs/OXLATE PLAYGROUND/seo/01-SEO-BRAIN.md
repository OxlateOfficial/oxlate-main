## SEO Philosophy
SEO is an architectural foundation, not an afterthought. For a new entity like Oxlate, credibility and visibility come from:
1. Complete machine readability and crawlability of primary copy.
2. Factual semantic entity clarity (`Organization` and `Service` types).
3. Legitimate first-party evidence (independent project case studies).
4. No fake "AI SEO" or "GEO" shortcuts (e.g. avoiding low-value keyword-stuffed city pages or bogus `llms.txt` files).
