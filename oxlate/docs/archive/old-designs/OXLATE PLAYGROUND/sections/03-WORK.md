# 03 — WORK SECTION — OXLATE V1

Version: 3.0  
Status: ACTIVE STRATEGIC AUTHORITY  
Last Updated: 2026-09-10

---

## 1. Purpose
Provides tangible visual and technical evidence of software craftsmanship and design execution while strictly upholding **The Truth Rule**.

---

## 2. Intended User Feeling
Authenticity, honesty, and tangible proof of capability. The visitor should feel:
> *"They are not trying to fool me with fake client logos or made-up metrics. They build real, high-quality software and present it with total transparency."*

---

## 3. Information Hierarchy
1. **Section Tag:** Monospace indicator `03 // SELECTED WORK`.
2. **Section Headline (H2):** "Demonstrations of craft."
3. **Disclosure Subtitle:** "A selection of independent products and technical systems built by the founding developer. These demonstrate our technical and design approach."
4. **Project Showcase Frame Archetype:**
   - *Honesty Badge (Required):* `Independent Project` or `Founding Developer's Work`.
   - *Title & Summary:* Project title and system purpose.
   - *Visual Media Slot:* High-resolution interface photography or architectural UI capture.
   - *Technical Architecture Notes:* Stack components, architectural decisions, and performance benchmarks.

---

## 4. Visual Direction (`RECOMMENDATION`)
- **Editorial Presentation:** Projects are framed like architectural case studies or magazine entries, not generic agency thumbnail cards.
- **Card Framing:** Parchment fill (`#FAF8F5`), Pale Stone border (`#D9D4CC`), sharp `2px–6px` corner radii, generous internal padding.
- **Grid Layout:** 2-column or 1-column large editorial frames on desktop; single-column stacked frames on mobile.

---

## 5. Possible Elements
- Editorial project showcase card (`PortfolioCard`)
- Strict disclosure badge (`Badge` variant `outline` / `solid-tint`)
- High-resolution interface capture framing (`L3 Media`)
- Technical stack metadata chips (`Geist Mono`)
- External repository or live demo link action

---

## 6. Animation Intention
- **Frame Entrance:** Soft upward viewport fade as user scrolls into Section 03.
- **Media Hover:** Subtle image scale (`scale-[1.01]` max); zero dramatic zooms or distorted tilt effects.

---

## 7. SEO Requirements
- Semantic `<h2>` and `<h3>` tags for project titles.
- Image `alt` tags providing detailed descriptions of interface elements.
- Schema alignment for showcase items (`CreativeWork` / `SoftwareApplication`).

---

## 8. Open Creative Questions (`UNDECIDED`)
- **Actual Work Inventory:** Specific projects, titles, and screenshots to feature in V1 are `UNDECIDED` and require owner selection (tracked in `DECISIONS-REQUIRED.md` Decision 04).
- **No Invented Content Rule:** Zero fake client names or fabricated performance metrics (`+43% revenue`) may be written into the playground or codebase.
