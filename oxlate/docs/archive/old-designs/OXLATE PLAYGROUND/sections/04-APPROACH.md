# 04 — APPROACH SECTION — OXLATE V1

Version: 3.0  
Status: ACTIVE STRATEGIC AUTHORITY  
Last Updated: 2026-09-10

---

## 1. Purpose
Explains Oxlate's engineering philosophy and proves why working directly with a small, founder-led technology partner delivers superior software quality than hiring a bloated agency with layers of account managers.

---

## 2. Intended User Feeling
Trust in technical discipline and builder proximity. The visitor should realize:
> *"The people designing and engineering my product are directly hands-on with the code. There is no loss of intent in translation."*

---

## 3. Information Hierarchy
1. **Section Tag:** Monospace indicator `04 // ENGINEERING APPROACH`.
2. **Section Headline (H2):** "We keep the build close."
3. **The 4 Foundational Principles:**
   - *01 — Understand the Problem First:* Map real user workflows and business requirements before writing a single line of code.
   - *02 — Design Before Overbuilding:* Establish state models, type contracts, and component architecture early to prevent structural refactors.
   - *03 — Build Only What the Product Needs:* Ruthlessly eliminate unnecessary dependencies, bloated frameworks, and premature complexity.
   - *04 — Keep the Final System Understandable:* Prioritize maintainability, clean type signatures, and clear documentation so software survives long-term.
