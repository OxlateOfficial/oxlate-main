# 06 — CONTACT SECTION — OXLATE V1

Version: 3.0  
Status: ACTIVE STRATEGIC AUTHORITY  
Last Updated: 2026-09-10

---

## 1. Purpose
The primary commercial conversion point. Invites inquiries with calm confidence rather than aggressive sales pressure or desperate countdown timers.

---

## 2. Intended User Feeling
Invited, respected, and clear. The visitor should feel:
> *"Starting a conversation with Oxlate is straightforward, low-pressure, and direct."*

---

## 3. Information Hierarchy
1. **Section Tag:** Monospace indicator `06 // ENGAGEMENT`.
2. **Section Headline (H2):** "Have something worth building?"
3. **Invitation Subtitle:** "Tell us what you are trying to build. We work directly with founders and teams on web and mobile products."
4. **Primary Conversion Trigger:** Rectangular bronze button ("Start a conversation" / "Contact") triggering direct communication.

---

## 4. Visual Direction (`RECOMMENDATION`)
- **Composition:** Focused architectural conversion panel with generous vertical whitespace.
- **Materiality:** Warm Ivory background (`#F5F2EC`), sharp Pale Stone top datum rule (`#D9D4CC`), solid Bronze CTA button (`#A87445`).
- **No Friction:** Avoid multi-step forms or mandatory phone number inputs on initial view.

---

## 5. Possible Elements
- Monospace chapter coordinate (`06 // ENGAGEMENT`)
- Primary Bronze CTA button (`Button` variant `primary`)
- Direct contact mailto trigger (`mailto:oxlateofficial@gmail.com` / `mailto:hello@oxlate.dev`)

## 6. Animation Intention

- **Entrance:** Soft opacity fade on viewport entry.
