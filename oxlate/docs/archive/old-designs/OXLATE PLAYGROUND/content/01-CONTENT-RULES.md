# CONTENT RULES — OXLATE V1

Version: 1.0  
Status: ACTIVE  
Last Updated: 2026-09-10

## Writing Imperatives
- **Declarative & Calm:** State what Oxlate builds simply and confidently.
- **No Exclamation Marks:** Prohibited across all body copy and headlines.
- **No Buzzword Stacking:** Avoid phrases like "leveraging cutting-edge AI-powered synergies".
- **No Fake Metrics:** Do not fabricate numbers such as "100+ happy clients", "43% conversion boost", or "99.9% uptime".
- **Honest Work Labeling:** Label independent work as `Independent Project` or `Founding Developer's Work`.
