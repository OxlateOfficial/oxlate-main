## 1. The Justification Rule (`DECIDED`)

Every visual mark on the page must have a structural reason to exist:
- **A Line?** Must align, divide, or anchor content.
- **A Crosshair (`+`)?** Must denote a coordinate intersection.
- **A Number?** Must indicate chapter hierarchy or sequence.
- **A Frame?** Must define spatial content boundaries.
- **Bronze?** Must denote active state, active focus, or direct conversion action.

If an element has no structural reason to exist, remove it.

---

## 2. Disallowed Decorative Patterns (`DECIDED`)

The following decorative trends dilute the calm architectural identity and are strictly prohibited:
- Colorful gradients, mesh gradients, or neon blurs.
- Floating glassmorphic cards or heavy fuzzy drop shadows.
- Fake real-time telemetry counters ("ACTIVE 60 FPS", "99.9% UPTIME").
- Toy interactive drawing pads, 3D model spinners, or physics widgets.
- Stock team photography, corporate handshakes, or AI-generated people.
