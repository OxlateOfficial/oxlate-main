## 1. Structural Primitives (`DECIDED`)

Primitives are fundamental, non-interactive geometric marks that establish architectural order across the canvas:

1. **Datum Scoring Rule (`Line`):** `1px` Pale Stone stroke (`#D9D4CC`). Marks section boundaries and anchors typographic compositions.
2. **Hairline Drafting Grid (`Grid`):** Subtly rendered background column lines (0.5px to 1px stroke at 10–18% opacity), simulating technical drafting paper (`HERO-SVG-01`).
3. **Alignment Crosshair (`Tick`):** Fine `+` marks positioned at primary column and datum intersections to signal CAD precision.
4. **Monospace Chapter Index (`Index`):** Uppercase monospace numeric indicator (`01 // INTRO`, `02 // CAPABILITIES`) establishing narrative sequence.
5. **Bronze Focal Pip (`Dot`):** `4px–6px` circular bronze mark (`#A87445`) denoting active state in side navigation or section tags.

---

## 2. Rule of Restraint (`DECIDED`)

Visual primitives must never be applied as random decorative noise. Every line, crosshair, or dot must anchor, divide, or align actual content.
