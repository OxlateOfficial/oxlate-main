---

## 1. What the Website Must Accomplish (`DECIDED`)

The primary objective of the Oxlate website is to establish immediate technical and aesthetic credibility for a new technology firm without leaning on manufactured pedigree.

- **Win on Perceived Discipline:** When a visitor leaves the site, their reaction must be: *"They haven't been around forever, but they clearly know how to construct software."*
- **A Digital Introduction, Not an Agency Funnel:** It must read like an intentional architectural monograph introducing the company's capabilities, not an aggressive "hire us" sales pitch.
- **Immediate Value Communication:** Within roughly 10–20 seconds of arriving, any visitor must effortlessly understand:
  1. What Oxlate is (A focused technology partner).
  2. What Oxlate builds (Purpose-built web experiences, web applications, and mobile products).
  3. What the actual work looks like (Transparently labeled technical systems).
  4. How Oxlate approaches building (Founder-led engineering discipline).
  5. How to start a project (Low-friction direct conversation).
- **Nothing Important Is Hidden:** Visitors must not need to hunt through obscure menus or wait through long cinematic sequences to find basic answers.

---

## 2. Audience Targets & Dual Intent (`DECIDED`)

The website must simultaneously answer two distinct buyer motivations:

1. **Indian SMB Founders & Growing Businesses:**
   - *Core Anxiety:* "Why do I need a bespoke web or mobile build instead of a cheap template?"
   - *Website Answer:* Your digital presence is the primary filter through which high-value customers judge your legitimacy. A purpose-built, high-performing product elevates your business stature immediately.
2. **International Founders & Engineering Teams:**
   - *Core Anxiety:* "Why should I trust a new, small technology partner over an established agency?"
   - *Website Answer:* We operate without middle management, bloated retainers, or junior hand-offs. The senior builders are directly hands-on with the code, ensuring structural integrity, speed, and clean architecture.

---

## 3. Commercial Scope (`DECIDED`)

- **Primary Core:**
  - Purpose-built websites and web applications (React, Next.js, TypeScript).
  - Mobile applications (Android, iOS, React Native).
- **Secondary Spectrum:**
  - Custom internal software, operational dashboards, and business systems where off-the-shelf software fails.

---

## 4. Explicit Non-Goals (`DECIDED`)

- **No Enterprise IT Pretension:** Never claim 500 imaginary consultants, global offices, or enterprise certifications Oxlate does not possess.
- **No SaaS / Tool Pitching:** Do not dilute the focus by pitching unreleased SaaS products, marketing automation, or AI wrapper experiments.
- **No Fabricated Proof:** Never display placeholder client logos, fake metrics ("43% revenue lift"), or synthetic testimonials.
