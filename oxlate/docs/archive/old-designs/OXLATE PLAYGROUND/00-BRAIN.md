## 2. Why It Looks This Way (The Architectural Premise)
Most digital agency websites rely on aggressive marketing funnels, flashy decorative trends, and fabricated proof. 

Oxlate communicates capability through **structural discipline**:
- The website is approached as an **architectural document** or **precision instrument**.
- The visual impact comes from **proportion, generous whitespace, typographic scale, and structural alignment**.
- Every visual line or marker exists to define structure or orient the reader.

---

## 3. Core Principles & Truth Rule (`DECIDED`)
1. **User Clarity:** The visitor understands what Oxlate offers and how to get in touch within seconds.
2. **Materiality over Decoration:** Warm Ivory atmosphere (`#F5F2EC`), Deep Black ink (`#111111`), Graphite structure (`#292929`), and rare Bronze accent (`#A87445`).
3. **Restraint as a Luxury Signal:** Generous whitespace, disciplined typography, and minimal decorative noise.
4. **The Truth Rule:** Never fabricate clients, testimonials, metrics, team headcounts, office locations, awards, or experience claims. Independent projects are presented strictly as technical capability demonstrations.
5. **No Arbitrary Data:** Never include fake technical coordinates, random numbers, or simulated telemetry. Structural markers must have authentic design purpose.
6. **Responsive Integrity:** Desktop and mobile are distinct, intentional compositions sharing the same design language.
