## 1. Absolute Invariants (`DECIDED`)

- **The Truth Rule:** Never fabricate client logos, testimonials, revenue metrics, or team headcounts. Independent work must carry an explicit disclosure badge: `Independent Project` or `Founding Developer's Work`.
- **Palette Lockdown:** Warm Ivory (`#F5F2EC`), Soft Cream (`#ECE8E1`), Parchment (`#FAF8F5`), Deep Black (`#111111`), Graphite (`#292929`), Neutral Slate (`#6C6B66`), Pale Stone (`#D9D4CC`), and Restrained Bronze (`#A87445`).
- **Bronze Restraint:** Bronze occupies 2% to 5% of visible UI area strictly. Used for primary CTA, active navigation indicators, and coordinate micro-accents. Never use bronze as a section or card background.
- **Tectonic Geometry:** Radii are sharp and intentional (`0px` on section boundaries, `2px–6px` on cards and project frames, `2px–4px` on buttons). Large 16px–24px rounded bubble cards and generic pill cards are strictly prohibited.
- **Text Machine-Readability & SEO:** All primary copy must exist as crawlable semantic HTML text. Essential copy must never be embedded inside WebGL canvas, SVG path coordinates, or animated image pixels.
- **Zero Scroll-Jacking on Mobile:** Mobile portrait screens (`< 768px`) must scroll naturally with touch inertia. Multi-stage scroll-pinning that traps touch scrolling is banned on mobile.

---

## 2. Agent Creative Freedom (`RECOMMENDATION`)

- The agent has creative freedom to adjust fine layout spacing, typographic sizing, and component alignment to optimize visual balance across viewports.
- The agent is expected to craft dedicated mobile portrait compositions from the shared design vocabulary rather than scaling down desktop views.

---

## 3. Decision Protocol**

- **Minor Layout & Spacing Adjustments:** Use design judgement within the architectural rules.
- **Brand System / Structural Changes:** Document in `CREATIVE-RECOMMENDATIONS.md` or `DECISIONS-REQUIRED.md` for owner review.
