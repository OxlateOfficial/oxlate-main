## Primary Buyer Segments

### 1. Indian SMB Founders & Growing Businesses
- **Core Question:** "Why do I need a purpose-built web/mobile product rather than a cheap off-the-shelf template?"
- **Answer:** A deliberate, fast, well-crafted digital product builds immediate trust, enhances professional stature, and drives serious customer inquiries.

### 2. International Startups & Product Teams
- **Core Question:** "Why should I trust a new technology partner over an established agency?"
- **Answer:** Direct involvement of senior engineering talent, transparent communication, modern tech stack (React/Next.js/React Native), zero tech debt, and lower overhead.
