## Positioning Statement
Oxlate is an architectural, precision technology partner building thoughtful web experiences, web applications, and mobile products for ambitious startups and businesses.

## What Oxlate Is
- A dedicated, high-craft engineering wing.
- Direct founder-to-code execution without agency bloat or account managers.
- Focused on software longevity, maintainability, and clean code architecture.

## What Oxlate Is Not
- Not a low-cost volume agency churning out WordPress templates.
- Not a massive enterprise consulting vendor pretending to have 500 offshore employees.
- Not a SaaS product company or automated AI service wrapper.
