## 1. Architectural Vocabulary of Form (`DECIDED`)

Oxlate expresses its brand identity through four signature architectural motifs:

### 1. Structural Framing & Elevation Linework
- **Form:** Ultra-fine hairline rules enclosing content columns with corner crosshair ticks (`+`).
- **Purpose:** Gives sections the structural geometry of an architectural blueprint or elevation drawing.

### 2. The Oxlate Structural Strut
- **Form:** Diagonal geometric linework derived from the structural arms of the Oxlate vector mark (`Oxlate_logoX_blk.svg`).
- **Purpose:** Guides the eye downward through section transitions while reinforcing the brand geometry.

### 3. Precision Coordinate & Measurement Notation
- **Form:** Uppercase monospace metadata tags (`01 // INTRO`, `SEC-02 // CAPABILITIES`) placed along structural boundaries.
- **Purpose:** Expresses engineering rigor and deliberate composition.

### 4. Threshold Diagonal Service Datum Beam (`RECOMMENDATION` / `UNDECIDED`)
- **Form:** The grey diagonal band from `HeroRoughIdea.png` cutting cleanly across the boundary between Section 01 and Section 02.
- **Purpose:** Acts as a structural transition bridge introducing capability disciplines (`Web Development • Mobile Applications • Custom Systems`) without cluttering the initial hero viewport.
